SkyrimNet MultiProxy v2.4.0 — your Google subscription, in Skyrim!

MultiProxy connects SkyrimNet to the AI of your choice — NPC dialogue, actions, diaries, bios, GameMaster — and starts itself whenever you play.

**🔗 Guide + download:** <https://github.com/awesmdiver/skyrimnet-multiproxy>

**🆕 New in v2.4.0**
- **Already pay for Google AI Pro or Ultra? Skyrim can use it.** Sign in once with your Google account — no API key, nothing extra to pay per reply, just like Claude and ChatGPT already work here.
- **More than Gemini under that one login.** The dashboard shows whatever models your account can run, and right now that includes Claude and GPT-OSS models alongside Google's own. Pick from the list.
- **Prefer pay-as-you-go?** A Google AI Studio key works too, and it's the faster of the two.

**✨ 10 AIs to choose from:** Claude, ChatGPT and Google AI Pro (subscriptions, no keys), plus OpenRouter, GLM, Nano-GPT, OpenAI, Gemini, Kilo Gateway, and free local models with Ollama. Mix and match — a different AI for different parts of SkyrimNet if you like.

**🛠️ Getting started:** extract the zip, run `setup.bat`, install the plugin in Vortex or MO2, then open the dashboard in your browser and add a key or sign in. The README walks through it with screenshots.

*Already on v2.3.0?* Only the proxy changed this time — swap in the new file and keep everything else as it is.

**⚙️ You'll need:** Skyrim SE, SkyrimNet, SKSE64, and one AI to talk to — a Claude, ChatGPT or Google login, an API key, or Ollama on your PC.

**🔀 Overlap:** built on Galanx's Claude-SkyrimNet-Proxy and rhinos0608's skyrimnet-codex-proxy. Run only one proxy on port 8000.
