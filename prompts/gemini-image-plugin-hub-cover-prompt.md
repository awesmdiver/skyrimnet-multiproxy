plugin's Discord thread). Same Nordic-fantasy family as the README and release banners.

**Hub rules:** one 16:9 PNG or JPEG, 640–2048 px wide, under 5 MB. It shows whole on fateless.ai, but
**Discord's forum card crops it to a square, so everything important must sit in the middle square.**
Reviewed by SkyrimNet's team: no real people, no ads, no links, no QR codes.
