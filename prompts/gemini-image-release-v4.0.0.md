# Release image: v4.0.0 — many stones, many voices

For the v4.0.0 GitHub release notes, the Plugin Hub cover and the Discord post. Same Nordic-fantasy family as the earlier
banners (the rune stone and the rainbow streams of light), with a new scene for what 4.0 is about.

The director's brief (2026-10-02): "want to stick with the rainbow streams, but want a single pillar in the middle, and
other pillars around it, with a single stream going to it - doesn't have to be in the village any more, could just be
outdoors or a different village. And around a couple of the pillars, people talking - so it incorporates multi-server
(pillars) and Higgs audio (talking)."

What it has to say at a glance: **one hub, several other machines working for it (the pillars), and voices (people
talking)**.

**Hub rules (it doubles as the Plugin Hub cover):** one 16:9 PNG or JPEG, 640 to 2048 px wide, under 5 MB. Discord's forum
card crops it to a square, so everything important sits in the middle square. No real people, no ads, no links, no text.

```
A dark, moody Skyrim-style Nordic fantasy scene, photorealistic and cinematic, wide 16:9 composition, matching this
project's existing banners. A wide snowy highland clearing at night, outdoors, ringed by dark pines and distant
mountains. At dead center stands one tall ancient carved standing stone covered in glowing blue Nordic runes: the hub.
Around it, at a respectful distance in a loose ring, stand four or five smaller carved rune stones, each glowing faintly
in its own color. From each smaller stone a single clean ribbon of light, each a different color (gold, teal, violet,
emerald, crimson), arcs gently through the air into the central stone, so the ribbons together read as a rainbow of
```
