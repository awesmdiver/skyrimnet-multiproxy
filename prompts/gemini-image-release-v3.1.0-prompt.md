# Release image: v3.1.0 — Ready for Beta 25

For the v3.1.0 GitHub release notes and release body. Same scene and style as the v2.3.0 through
v3.0.0 release images, so the set reads as one family.

This release's flagship (per the director): MultiProxy is ready for SkyrimNet Beta 25, whose headline
is smarter routing. When one AI stalls, SkyrimNet switches to a backup. Visual idea: one stream of
light falters, and another smoothly takes over, so the village fire never goes dark.

```
A dark, moody Skyrim-style Nordic fantasy scene, photorealistic and cinematic, wide 16:9 banner
composition, matching this project's existing README banner: an ancient carved standing stone in a
snowy mountain clearing at night, covered in glowing blue Nordic runes, acting as a hub. Rivers of
soft colored light flow in from the misty distance and converge into the stone, and warm light
carries on from it to a Nordic village on the right where cloaked figures talk around a fire under
lantern-lit windows. Green and violet aurora borealis across the sky, falling snow, cold blue shadows
against warm firelight. One stream of light on the left flickers and breaks apart into fading embers
partway to the stone, and right beside it a second, steady golden stream smoothly branches off and
carries on into the stone in its place, so the flow never stops. In the foreground stands a tall
carved stone plaque upright in the snow, engraved in bold crisp fantasy-serif capitals reading
exactly: "BETA 25". Give the plaque enough width for the text to sit large and uncramped. Across the
top, engraved in large glowing Nordic lettering: "READY FOR BETA 25". Every piece of lettering must be
large, crisp and correctly spelled, with no other text anywhere in the image. No real-world company
logos, brand marks, computers, screens, or modern technology anywhere.
```
