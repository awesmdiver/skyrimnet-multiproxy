# Release image: v3.0.0 — A speedometer for your AI

For the v3.0.0 GitHub release notes (`RELEASE_NOTES.md`, top of the new section) and the release
body. Same scene and style as the v2.3.0, v2.4.0, and v2.5.0 release images so the set reads as one
family.

This release's flagship (per the director): the new Speeds tab, which shows how fast every AI you've
connected is actually answering, live, labeled with what SkyrimNet is using it for. Vision support
also landed this release, but it's a fix, not the headline — keep it unlabeled in the background like
previous releases treated a real but secondary addition.

```
A dark, moody Skyrim-style Nordic fantasy scene, photorealistic and cinematic, wide 16:9 banner
composition, matching this project's existing README banner: an ancient carved standing stone in a
snowy mountain clearing at night, covered in glowing blue Nordic runes, acting as a hub. Rivers of
soft colored light flow in from the misty distance and converge into the stone, and warm light
carries on from it to a Nordic village on the right where cloaked figures talk around a fire under
lantern-lit windows. Green and violet aurora borealis across the sky, falling snow, cold blue shadows
against warm firelight. Eleven streams of light flow into the stone, but ONE of them moves visibly
faster than the rest — a warm amber-orange streak with a comet-like trailing blur, arriving from the
left with real motion and urgency compared to the other calm, steady streams — ending at its own tall
carved stone plaque standing upright in the snow in the foreground, engraved in bold crisp
fantasy-serif capitals reading exactly: "SPEEDS". Give the plaque enough width for the whole word to
sit large and uncramped. The other ten streams are dimmer, calmer, and unlabelled. Across the top,
engraved in large glowing Nordic lettering: "A SPEEDOMETER FOR YOUR AI". Every piece of lettering must
be large, crisp and correctly spelled, with no other text anywhere in the image. No real-world company
logos, brand marks, computers, screens, clocks, stopwatches, or modern technology anywhere.
```

Notes:

- Single plaque this time — v3.0.0 has one named flagship (the Speeds tab), same as v2.5.0's single-
  plaque layout.
- "A SPEEDOMETER FOR YOUR AI" is the top banner line — check it renders as five clean words, no
  dropped or merged letters, before accepting.
- The one fast-moving stream is the whole visual joke of this release — it should read as obviously
  quicker/more energetic than the other ten calm streams, not just brighter. If the motion-blur/comet
  effect doesn't render clearly, a re-roll is worth it this time (unlike past releases' "don't bother
  re-rolling the dim streams" note) — the speed motif IS the point of this one.
- No literal clock/stopwatch/hourglass imagery — keep the speed idea expressed through motion in the
  light itself, staying in the Nordic-fantasy visual language rather than reaching for modern icons.
- Once accepted: shrink to 1600px wide as `assets/release-banner-v3-0-0.jpg`, keep the full-res
  original beside it as `assets/release-banner-v3-0-0-original.jpg`, past the `assets/*` ignore rule.
