# Voice samples: credits and licences

The sample clips in this folder are **made-up voices**: a man and a woman described in words and made by AI. They are not a real person and not a voice from any game. They are included free of charge, only so people can hear what each voice sounds like in the **Which voice?** guide. They are never sold, and nobody is paid for them.
